Plain-language privacy policy

How CollectCare handles your health information

Last updated November 18, 2026. This is a draft written for the CollectCare demo. A healthcare attorney should review it before launch.

The short version

  • We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, data brokers, drug companies or anyone else.
  • Nothing connects without your OK. You choose each account to link, and you see exactly what we will pull in before you approve it.
  • You can turn it off any time. Disconnect any account in one tap. You can also delete everything we hold about you.
  • We only use your data to help you. We use it to show your benefits, care, bills and reminders. We don't use it for ads.
  • If something goes wrong, we tell you. If your information is ever exposed, we will notify you, and the FTC, as the law requires.

What we collect

  • Your insurance card details, plan and benefits
  • Your photo ID details: ID type, name, last 4 digits, expiration date and issuing state. We use these to confirm the insurance card belongs to you. We don't keep a copy of the ID photo.
  • Claims and what you have paid toward your deductible
  • Visits, diagnoses, medications, allergies and lab results from your doctors
  • Prescriptions and refills from your pharmacy
  • Appointments you book through CollectCare
  • Your account info: name, email, phone, and family members you add
  • Your location, only when you tap "Nearest urgent care" and allow it. We use it to sort the list by distance. We don't save it.

Where it comes from

  • You. For example, when you scan your card or type in your info.
  • Accounts you connect. These include your health plan, Medicare, your doctors' patient portals, your lab and your pharmacy. Each one uses the official patient access connections that federal rules require them to offer.
  • Your doctor's office. Only if your practice uses CollectCare, and only for check-in and scheduling.

Who we share with

We share your information only in these cases:

  • When you tell us to. For example, sharing your check-in code with a clinic, or giving a caregiver access.
  • Companies that help us run CollectCare. These include secure cloud hosting and identity checks. They must sign contracts that limit use to running CollectCare and require HIPAA-level protection.
  • When the law requires it. For example, a valid court order. We will tell you unless the law forbids it.

We never share your data for marketing, and we never sell it. We don't use your health data to train outside AI models.

Your choices and rights

  • See it. View everything we hold about you.
  • Download it. Get a copy in a standard format.
  • Fix it. Ask us to correct something that's wrong.
  • Disconnect. Unlink any account. We stop pulling new data right away.
  • Delete it. Close your account and we erase your data within 30 days, except what the law makes us keep.

Family and caregivers

  • Parents or guardians can manage a child's account until the child turns 18.
  • Adult family members, including a 19-year-old, must approve their own connections.
  • A caregiver, such as an adult child helping a parent on Medicare, can get access only with that person's permission or a legal healthcare proxy.
  • You can remove a caregiver at any time.

How we protect it

Encrypted

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Locked down

Accounts use two-step sign-in. Staff access is limited and logged.

Tested

We run regular security reviews and outside testing, and follow HIPAA Security Rule practices.

Which laws apply

When you use CollectCare on your own to collect your records, the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and California health privacy laws (CMIA and CCPA) protect your data. When CollectCare works for a doctor's office or health plan, HIPAA also applies. We sign a Business Associate Agreement with them.

Federal rules require health apps to tell you, in plain language, how they use and share your data before you connect. They also require apps to get your consent and let you revoke it. This page and our connect screen are built to do that. Learn more at HealthIT.gov and the FTC.

Questions

Email privacy@collectmycare.com or write to CollectCare Privacy, Redondo Beach, CA. We reply within 10 business days. If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will tell you first and ask again before using your data in a new way.
