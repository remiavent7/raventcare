Practice pilot program

Bring CollectCare to your practice

This is a free 90-day pilot for independent and group practices. Patients check in with a QR code. Your staff sees every room, every wait and every provider on one screen. See the practice view demo.

What you get

Live practice board

See rooms, the waiting room, provider lanes and today's schedule in real time, built for busy days like 10 rooms and 70 patients.

What you get

QR check-in

Patients scan at the front desk. Their insurance, copay and forms are already on their phone. That means fewer clipboards and shorter lines.

What you get

Patient app

Your patients see their benefits, costs, lab results, refills and appointments in one place. It's free for them.

What we need from you

  • Business Associate Agreement. We sign a BAA before any patient data is shared.
  • Security review. We complete your security questionnaire and share our test results.
  • EHR connection. We start read-only, with schedule and rooms. We work with the major EHR systems.
  • One pilot lead. An office manager who meets with us for 30 minutes a week.

Pilot timeline

  1. Weeks 1–2: BAA, security review, kickoff
  2. Weeks 3–4: Connect the schedule, set up rooms and the QR stand
  3. Weeks 5–12: Go live, with weekly check-ins on wait times and front-desk time
  4. Week 13: Results review, then you decide whether to continue

There's no cost and no long-term contract during the pilot.
