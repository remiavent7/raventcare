Provider details come from the CMS National Plan and Provider Enumeration System (NPPES), served through the National Library of Medicine Clinical Tables API. Distances are approximate and measured to the center of each ZIP code, using ZIP data from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

The registry lists who is licensed and where they practice. It does not confirm insurance networks, whether a provider is taking new patients, or office hours. Call the office to confirm before your visit.

Appointment requests you save are kept only on this device (in your browser) and are not sent to CollectCare or to the office. CollectCare can’t book directly with these offices yet, so every appointment is confirmed by calling the office.
