Your family's healthcare, in one private place

The CollectCare private beta is open by invitation to friends, family and investors. Your information is stored on a secure server, is visible only to your account and is never sold or used for ads.

  • Add each family member and keep their insurance, doctors, medications, allergies and documents together
  • Two-step sign-in, automatic sign-out and a full activity log
  • Download everything or delete your account at any time

Connecting Epic/MyChart, health plans and Medicare is coming next. In this beta you add information yourself. Looking for the investor walkthrough? Open the sample-data demo.
